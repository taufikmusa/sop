What This Guide Covers

This guide takes a brand-new Tekla Structures 2026 user from a freshly installed machine to a model open on screen. It stops there on purpose — modeling workflows, drawing production and numbering are separate guides.

STEP 01 · IDENTITY

Trimble Identity & Subscription

You need a Trimble Identity to download Tekla Structures and to use your subscriptions. The subscription itself is assigned to that identity by your company administrator before you can start.

STEP 02 · FIRST LAUNCH

Sign In and Pick Your Setup

On first start you sign in, confirm which licensing option to use, and choose the three settings that shape your whole session: environment, role and configuration.

STEP 03 · FIRST MODEL

Create the Model

Name the model, decide where it is stored, choose single-user or multi-user mode, then let Tekla Structures open the default model view.

Out of scope

Modeling workflow, drawing creation and numbering are deliberately not covered here. This guide ends the moment the first model view is open.

Prerequisites Before You Launch

ACCOUNT

A Trimble Identity

Required both to download the software and to use your subscription. Use your company email address — verification and sign-in codes are sent there.

ACCOUNT

Membership of the Company Account

Your identity must belong to your company's Tekla account. Accept the administrator's invitation from your Online Profile, ask for one, or submit a join request if your email domain matches.

LICENSING

A Subscription Assigned to You

Administrators assign subscriptions and access in the Tekla Online Admin Tool (admin.account.tekla.com). Once a subscription is activated for your Trimble Identity, you can select a license when you sign in.

SOFTWARE

Tekla Structures 2026 Installed

Install the software itself, plus at least one environment package. If no environment is installed, only the Blank environment will be offered at startup.

SECURITY

Access to Your Mailbox

Users get 2-step verification with one-time passcodes unless the organization has enabled multi-factor authentication. Without access to that mailbox, you cannot sign in.

LICENSING · LEGACY

On-Premises Server (Only If Applicable)

New customers normally use subscription (online) licensing. If your company runs an on-premises server, its tools must be at the 2025 versions or later — see the version notes below.

Step 1 — Create and Prepare Your Trimble Identity

  1. 1

    Check your inbox for an invitation

    When your company buys a Tekla subscription, the named contact receives an email invitation. Create your Trimble Identity using the link in that email and fill in all of the required user profile information.

  2. 2

    No invitation? Create the identity yourself

    Create a Trimble ID following Trimble's own account instructions, then open your Online Profile at account.tekla.com, set Profile type to Professional, and complete the mandatory fields.

  3. 3

    Join your company account

    Three routes are available: accept an administrator invitation from your Online Profile, ask your administrator to send one, or submit a join request if your email domain matches your company's.

    Warning / Troubleshooting

    Use a company email address you will keep access to. If you lose access to the mailbox used to create your Trimble Identity, you will not be able to sign in using 2-step verification.

  4. 4

    Have the administrator assign your subscription

    An administrator activates the subscription for your Trimble Identity in the Tekla Online Admin Tool. Only after that can you select a license when you sign in to Tekla Structures.

Two Ways to Start Tekla Structures

When you install Tekla Structures, two desktop icons appear: one opens the classic startup screen, the other opens the Tekla Launcher startup screen. Both lead to the same product — the difference is where you choose your setup and how models are managed.

OPTION A

Classic Startup Screen

You pick environment, role and configuration up front in the setup dialog, then reach a start screen with tabs for recent models, all models, shared models, and creating a new one. This is the familiar route for anyone who has used earlier versions.

OPTION B

Tekla Launcher

You choose your subscription once, then work from Models, Recent and New tabs. Version and environment are chosen per model, and the role is selected as the model opens.

Step 2A — First Launch via the Classic Startup Screen

  1. 1

    Start the application

    Double-click the Tekla Structures desktop icon, or select Tekla Structures from the Windows Start menu.

  2. 2

    Sign in with your Trimble Identity

    Sign in when prompted. This is the sign-in that connects Tekla Structures to the subscription assigned to you.

  3. 3

    Confirm the licensing option

    A dialog appears where you choose your Tekla Structures setup and the license to use. The default is the Tekla Structures subscription, which is what most new customers need. If your company uses an on-premises license server instead, click Change license server at the bottom of the dialog and select Use your on-premises license server.

    Warning / Troubleshooting

    If Tekla Structures starts with the wrong licensing method, you do not need to reinstall. Reopen the startup dialog, click Change license server, pick the correct option and click OK. On-premises setups on a separate machine require the license server address to be entered manually.

  4. 4

    Select the environment

    Choose the environment that matches the region your project is built in. You can also select the blank project and use it as the basis to create your own customized environment.

  5. 5

    Select the role

    Pick the role that matches the work you do. The roles typically available are Concrete Contractor, Engineer, General Contractor, Precast Concrete Detailer, Production Planner for Concrete, Rebar Detailer and Steel Detailer.

  6. 6

    Select the configuration

    Choose the configuration your subscription plan entitles you to — Diamond, Graphite or Carbon.

  7. 7

    Click OK to reach the start screen

    Click OK. The start screen opens with tabs for recent models, all models and shared models, plus the option to create a new one. You can search, sort and open models from here.

Step 2B — First Launch via Tekla Launcher

  1. 1

    Open Tekla Launcher

    Double-click the Tekla Launcher desktop icon, or select it from the Windows Start menu.

  2. 2

    Select your Tekla Structures subscription

    On first use a dialog appears where you select your Tekla Structures subscription. The subscription with the highest feature level is selected automatically; if you have more than one, you can switch between them.

  3. 3

    Go to the New tab

    Open the New tab, find the New blank model template, and click Create.

  4. 4

    Fill in the model details

    Enter a model name and description. Select an installed Tekla Structures version, or download a different one. Then choose an environment — the list is filtered by the version you selected.

  5. 5

    Apply optional settings

    If you are using Custom environments, select the upgrade behavior options. You can optionally apply company settings from the Project Settings Management Console, and add a thumbnail image for the model.

  6. 6

    Create the model and choose a role

    Click Create new. In the Role dialog, select a role that is used in the model and click Select. Tekla Structures starts with the model already open.

Good to know

To open an existing model from the Launcher, go to the Models tab, select the model and click Open (or double-click the row), then select a role and click Select. Models reopened from the Recent tab skip the role selection, so they open faster.

Understanding Environment, Role and Configuration

These three choices are the most common source of confusion on day one. They are independent of one another and they do different jobs.

WHAT YOU BUILD WITH

Environment

Defines the materials, grades, profiles, drawing settings, component settings and .ini file settings used in a specific locale. Several environments can be installed at once, and new ones can be added later.

WHAT YOU SEE

Role

A user designation, independent of your subscription or license, that makes the interface and settings clearer and faster for your task. Settings and filters are tailored to the role, and irrelevant object property preloads are hidden.

WHAT YOU OWN

Configuration

A product variant determined by your subscription plan — Tekla Structures Diamond, Graphite or Carbon. Your subscription or license decides which configurations you can access.

Warning / Troubleshooting

If the only environment offered is Blank, no environment package has been installed on the machine. The Blank environment gives you parametric profiles, undefined bolt, material and rebar grades, and basic drawing layouts — usable, but it is not a substitute for your regional environment. Install the correct environment before starting production work. Role setups are normally configured during installation by Trimble personnel, and administrators can create custom roles.

Step 3 — Create Your First Model

Create a separate model for each Tekla Structures project. Each model is stored in its own folder under the TeklaStructuresModels folder. These steps apply when you are already inside Tekla Structures — if you created the model from Tekla Launcher in Step 2B, it is already open and you can skip this section.

  1. 1

    Open the New model dialog

    On the File menu, click New.

  2. 2

    Name the model

    In the Name box, enter a name for the new model. The maximum length is 40 characters.

    Warning / Troubleshooting

    Do not use the special characters / \ ; : | in the model name. Decide on a permanent name now: the name can be changed afterwards, but doing so involves changing several file names.

  3. 3

    Define where the model is saved

    By default the model is saved in the TeklaStructuresModels folder created during installation. Click Browse to change the folder.

  4. 4

    Choose the operation mode

    Select Single-user for a model worked on by one person at a time, or Multi-user for a server-based model that several users work on simultaneously. For a first model, single-user is the straightforward choice.

  5. 5

    Optionally select a model template

    You can select a model template as the starting point, and mark templates as Favorite or Hidden to keep the list tidy.

  6. 6

    Optionally enable Trimble Connect collaboration

    Select Start Trimble Connect collaboration if you want the model linked to a Trimble Connect project once it has been created.

  7. 7

    Click Create

    Tekla Structures creates the model and opens the default model view. What you see in that view may differ depending on the model template you selected. At this point onboarding is complete — the modeling workflow begins here and is covered in separate guides.

Version Notes for Tekla Structures 2026

Topic What changed in 2026
Terminology"Organization" is now Account, and "external license user" is now Member. "Employee" now refers to a role rather than a membership status.
Inviting new usersPreviously new users defaulted to employee status. You must now select the employee role explicitly when inviting them. This affects access to company content in Tekla Warehouse and to model sharing.
On-premises licensingTekla Structures 2026 is not compatible with earlier versions of Tekla License Server, Tekla License Borrow Tool, or the Tekla On-Demand License Administration Tool. These must be upgraded to their 2025 versions.
Graphite configurationGraphite now supports detailing components in the same way as Diamond. Administrators can control this with the XS_USE_CONCEPTUAL_COMPONENTS_FOR_GRAPHITE advanced option, which defaults to FALSE.
Warning / Troubleshooting

If a new user cannot see company content in Tekla Warehouse or cannot use model sharing, check whether they were invited with the employee role selected. In 2026 this is no longer applied automatically.

First-Day Checklist

CHECK 01

Sign-in succeeded

You signed in with your Trimble Identity and a subscription was available to select. If no license appears, the administrator has not yet activated a subscription for your identity.

CHECK 02

Correct environment loaded

The environment you selected matches the region of the project, so materials, grades, profiles and drawing settings are the ones your project actually uses.

CHECK 03

Role matches your work

The role you picked reflects your discipline. The role is independent of the license, so choosing a different one does not change what you are entitled to use.

CHECK 04

Configuration is as expected

Diamond, Graphite or Carbon — confirm this matches the subscription plan your company purchased before you start relying on a feature.

CHECK 05

Model name is permanent

Maximum 40 characters, no / \ ; : |, and a name you are willing to keep — renaming later means changing several file names.

CHECK 06

Model view is open

The default model view has opened. Onboarding ends here; modeling, drawings and numbering are covered in their own guides.
